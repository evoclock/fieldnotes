New pieces, without checking back

There is no button a website can press to add itself to your reader, so this is the address to paste into one. After that, anything published here turns up on its own.

Or open the feed itself. It is XML, and it is meant for a reader rather than for you.

If you do not have a reader

Any of these will do, and all of them are free to start with:

  • NetNewsWire, for Mac and iOS.
  • Feedly or Inoreader, in a browser.
  • Thunderbird, if you would rather they arrived beside your email.

Most readers accept the site address on its own, so evoclock.github.io/fieldnotes is usually enough. The page declares where its feed lives and the reader follows that.
